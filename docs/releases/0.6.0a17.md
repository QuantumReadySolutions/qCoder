# qCoder 0.6.0a17

qCoder 0.6.0a17 is a behavior-changing pre-release successor to qCoder 0.6.0a16. It is not a
stable or generally available release, and package publication does not activate a named-client
support claim.

This exact candidate is immutable historical failure evidence. Its release-candidate disposition
was rejected after the native-Linux clarification-recovery failure, and it must not be published.
The note below preserves the behavior implemented in those bytes; it is not a publication or
qualification claim.

This release keeps active-loop source, circuit, and result currentness causally coherent. Replacing
an upstream artifact invalidates dependent downstream currentness without rewriting historical
revisions. Current result registration requires strict result manifests with sufficient circuit
lineage. Legacy bare counts fail closed, while a structurally valid result whose lineage is unknown
can be retained only as historical, non-current evidence.

The connected-assistant binding transports exact customer-selected artifact paths as structured
values. It does not infer additional paths, discover neighboring files, or broaden the selected
scope. A pre-existing selected source may satisfy the source role without being rewritten or
attributed to the assistant. Recovery may complete an already-authorized, already-run pending step
without authorizing another execution.

Exactly twelve public Context Bridge tools and the two private coordination operations remain.
qCoder does not gain authority to execute a circuit, simulator, runtime, or hardware job. Binding
and adapter behavior do not by themselves establish qualification for any client, client version,
operating environment, topology, or workstyle. This release makes no latency or quiet-operation
guarantee and does not establish general framework qualification.

Publication state, deployment state, and qualification claims remain separate from these immutable
package bytes.
